# Data Storytelling Templates

## Executive Narrative Template

```markdown
### Headline
[Metric] [improved/declined/remained stable] by [amount] during [period], mainly driven by [segment/driver].

### What happened
- [Finding 1 with value and comparison]
- [Finding 2 with value and comparison]
- [Finding 3 with value and comparison]

### Why it matters
[Business impact tied to cost, revenue, customer experience, SLA, risk, capacity, or quality.]

### Decision needed
[The specific executive decision or tradeoff.]

### Recommended action
[Owner-ready next step], expected to affect [follow-up KPI] by [timeframe].
```

## Finding-to-Decision Template

```text
Finding: [Metric] changed from [baseline] to [current] in [period].
Evidence: [Chart/segment/calculation] shows [proof].
Driver: The movement is concentrated in [driver].
Impact: This creates [business risk/opportunity].
Decision: Leaders should decide whether to [choice].
Action: [Team/owner] should [next step] and monitor [follow-up KPI].
```

## Insight Severity Rules

- `critical`: KPI breached threshold and requires immediate executive decision.
- `high`: KPI is materially off target or deteriorating across multiple periods.
- `medium`: KPI moved meaningfully but impact is localized or reversible.
- `low`: KPI is stable, informational, or early signal only.

## Strong Executive Language

Prefer:

- "The decision is..."
- "The risk is concentrated in..."
- "The leading indicator suggests..."
- "The fastest corrective action is..."
- "Monitor recovery through..."

Avoid:

- "It is interesting that..."
- "The data shows many things..."
- "Further analysis may be needed" without saying what analysis and why.
- Generic recommendations that do not name an owner, lever, or follow-up KPI.
